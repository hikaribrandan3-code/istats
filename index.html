Frequently Asked Questions

How much does iStats cost?
iStats is $4.99 — a one-time purchase with no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no recurring charges. Buy once, use forever. All features are included. Alternatively, iStats is included free as an add-on when you purchase the iSuite bundle.
Does it work on Intel Macs?
iStats is optimized for Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) and requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Intel Mac support is not available at this time.
Does iStats collect any data?
Absolutely not. iStats runs entirely on your Mac using native system APIs. No network calls, no telemetry, no accounts required. Your system data never leaves your machine.

Yes. iStats is part of the iSuite Office family of Mac apps, alongside iBrain and others — each sold separately so you only pay for what you need.
